How it works

A simple process from complaint to resolution.

1

Report

Choose a category, describe the problem and submit your complaint.

2

Review

The hostel authority checks the complaint and assigns it to the concerned staff.

3

Track

Students can check the current status of their complaint.

4

Resolve

After the issue is fixed, the complaint is marked resolved and feedback can be given.
